## STiBaRC

The STiBaRC Team is a small group of ambitious developers focused on creating various things on the web; just for the thrill and excitement of being able to share our bizzare creations. Launching our namesake social media service in 2017, we have since ventured into other projects—from blogging service Thinkblog and short-form video platform STiBaRC Clips to our own in-house record label STiBaRC Records. Led by founder and CEO Joshua Herron, the STiBaRC Team strives to create and develop products in our own unique, wacky way.
